GigaCrowd Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

GigaCrowd ("the app," "we," "us") is a live-audience crowd energy tool for performers and their viewers. This page explains what data GigaCrowd collects, how it's used, and your choices.

What we collect

When you join a show, GigaCrowd's microphone feature reads how loud your device's microphone is and sends only an anonymous numeric volume level (0-100) to our server, roughly ten times per second while you're actively connected. That number is used to compute the show's live "crowd energy" for everyone in that same show.

We never record, store, or transmit your actual voice or any audio. No audio ever leaves your device — only the numeric volume level.

GigaCrowd does not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information to use the app.

Like any app or website, standard technical data (such as your IP address and general device/browser information) is processed automatically by our hosting provider as part of normal internet connections. We do not use this data for tracking, advertising, or analytics, and GigaCrowd does not include any third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

How your data is used

The volume level you send is used solely to calculate the live crowd energy for the specific show (room) you joined, and to drive the audio/visual effects performers see and hear. It is never used for any other purpose.

Data retention

Volume levels are held only in server memory for as long as you're actively connected to a show. Nothing is written to a database or retained after you disconnect or the show ends. When a show ends, all its data is discarded.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. Our backend infrastructure is hosted by Railway (a cloud hosting provider), which may process standard connection-level technical data as part of providing that hosting service, under its own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke microphone access at any time through your device's system settings, which stops GigaCrowd from reading any volume data. Since GigaCrowd doesn't create accounts or store personal data tied to you, there is no persistent personal data to request access to or deletion of beyond simply not using the app.

Children's privacy

GigaCrowd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
